## [1.8.0] - 2026-10-23

Check image file/pixel/memory budgets before decode, bound generated sizes, validate real thumbnail paths and avoid same-name duplicate copies. Add no new schema, cron, automated scan or storefront assets.
- Harden taxonomy assignment capabilities, scheduled-rule/filter whitelists, CSV formulas with leading whitespace/control characters and literal roundtrips, XML address limits, unsafe external URLs, and mandatory valid SHA-256/size metadata plus download verification for updates. Preserve WordPress cookie-nonce authorization, role and history ownership checks; add targeted regressions rather than custom authentication.
